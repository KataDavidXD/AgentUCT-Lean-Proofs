# Source and rights

This public companion retains the mathematical core first archived on
8 October 2026 and its English core reading edition. The source release of
18 October 2026 also includes the existing supplementary finite-budget
construction described in arXiv v3. The 133 supplementary modules are the
minimal project import closure of `WindowFiniteBudget`,
`SavedInitialPriorBoundary` and `NativeConcreteReporting`. They add 3,307
public theorems to the modular core's 385, without changing their original
statements or proof bodies. The English core edition continues to cover 385.

The publication reference is
[arXiv:2607.24162v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24162v3), including its
supplement. The added sources cover the disclosed 13M conditional examples,
per-query quote projection, specified complete 32M continuations and initial
price boundary. The exact assumptions and conclusions are the Lean
declarations. They do not establish Python floating-point, PRNG or live-service
refinement, all legal completion tapes, or universal finite-budget superiority.
The benchmark's validation-selected test endpoint and full phase costs are
separate from the construction's best-training-quality/search-cost objective.

The [Python allocation core](https://github.com/KataDavidXD/AgentUCT) is a
separate component. The research workspace is maintained separately; it is
not a publication source to synchronize wholesale into either companion.

The current tree omits old standalone reading PDFs, manuscript copies,
historical logs, internal verification records and vendored authoring tools.
Fresh checks produce ignored local output; no internal log or machine path is
needed to reproduce them. The published text is available
from arXiv. Earlier repository content remains in Git history; the cleanup
does not erase prior disclosures.

Original research materials retain their authors' existing rights. Public
availability does not grant a new software or manuscript license. External
dependency licenses are listed in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
