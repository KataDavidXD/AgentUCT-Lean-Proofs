\section{Related Work}
UCT applies bandit allocation recursively to a tree \citep{kocsis2006bandit}, where adaptive descendants change an ancestor's return sequence. UCB1's stationary-bandit guarantee \citep{auer2002finite} therefore does not directly establish a tree rate. Coquelin and Munos expose severe depth-dependent UCT transients \citep{coquelin2007bandit}; Shah, Xie, and Xu analyze recursive nonstationarity with a polynomial bonus in discounted MDP planning \citep{shah2020mcts}. Our fixed-table analysis retains the logarithmic tree bonus and derives subtree deficits under bounded cold prices and persistent execution.

Pipeline optimizers already incorporate reusable computation into acquisition.
FLASH combines cost-sensitive expected improvement with prefix caching and LRU
eviction \citep{zhang2016flash}. EEIPU uses stage-cost Gaussian processes and
inverse remaining cost for selected memoized prefixes \citep{essofi2024memoization}.
MSBO maintains partial cascade states and can start or advance an evaluation
using stage models and proxy observations \citep{torresi2026multistage}.
AgentUCT connects reuse to complete terminal returns backed up through a tree.
Its demand-profile result characterizes the residual-cost information sufficient
for a family of query panels, while its rate theorem follows adaptive ancestor
statistics and shared persistent publications.

LaMBO represents modular switching costs through a tree and combines a slowly
moving bandit with local Bayesian optimization, proving switching-cost-regularized
regret \citep{lin2021lambo}. Its cost depends on the preceding configuration.
AgentUCT can restore a prefix paid for at any earlier evaluation, and its
allocation uses terminal-return means over expanded ancestors.

AFlow searches generated programs through edits, execution feedback and a
score-based/uniform parent-selection mixture \citep{zhang2024aflow}. Our exploratory
pilot shares the evaluator and proposal mechanism across bounded adaptations
of that policy and AgentUCT. Metareasoning values a final decision against
computation cost \citep{hay2012selecting}; this motivates reporting endpoint
quality and full optimization cost alongside cumulative-quality theory.
