BACKUP-01: operational reward backup equals the selected-return statistics.

An evaluation event contains the expanded tree prefix, its terminal completion,
the terminal raw Score, and the actual bill. A successful complete evaluation
updates each prefix of the expanded node exactly once, and no other tree node.
The bill is stored separately and does not change raw reward statistics.

Start from zero visits and zero reward_sum at every node. Replay events by a
left fold of the actual update. For every node, prove that visits equal the
number of events whose expanded path passes through that node, and reward_sum
equals the sum of their terminal raw Scores. Consequently Q is their mean,
using Q=0 for unvisited nodes. The root receives every completed evaluation.
If all terminal Scores lie in [0,1], every backed-up Q remains in [0,1].

The expanded prefix must be a prefix of the terminal completion; it need not
equal the terminal path. Rollout-only states are not inserted into the tree.
No independence, concentration, regret, or optimality claim is part of BACKUP-01.
Tree growth and index selection will be connected in subsequent declarations.
