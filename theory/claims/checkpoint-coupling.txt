CHECKPOINT-01: the resource ledger and deepest restorable suffix agree when prefixes persist.

Define the deepest checkpoint by maximizing saved stage indices for the queried
qid and full path, including index zero for the uncached root. Prove existence,
membership, maximality, and the path-length bound directly from this definition.

For arbitrary sparse caches, only stages beyond this checkpoint are executed;
do not replace this suffix by independently testing every ancestor object.
Under prefix completeness (every nonempty prefix of a saved checkpoint is also
saved), prove that a stage is missing iff its index is greater than the deepest
checkpoint. Then prove exact equality between the qid-prefix set-difference
bill and the suffix sum, using injectivity of nonempty path prefixes by length.

Prove prefix completeness is preserved by the full successful materialization
transition in EXECUTION-01. Thus K=M is a derived valid invariant in that main
model, rather than an assumption hidden in the cost identity. Sparse warm starts
need their own covered-prefix initial ledger or the explicit suffix semantics.
