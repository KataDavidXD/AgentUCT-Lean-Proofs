RATE-ALGEBRA-01: numerical and finite counting steps for the tree induction.

Derive a concrete visit target m=ceil(max(1,(32*A/delta)^2,8*B/delta))
such that any bound D <= A*log(N+1)+B gives D/N <= delta/4 for N>=m.
Given an optimal child's backed mean within delta/4 of its subtree maximum,
raw means below their own maxima, zero/common cost shift, and actual index
maximization, derive the selected positive-gap child's prior-count cap
4*c^2*log(parent+1)/delta^2. Count early and late selections explicitly.
These are induction steps; they do not yet assume or assert a full-tree rate.
