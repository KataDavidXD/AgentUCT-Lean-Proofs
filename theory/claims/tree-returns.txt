RETURNS-01: adaptive ancestor means are exact subtree-deficit averages.

Fix a finite set of terminal workflows and a fixed raw reward table for the full
panel. Define V(p) as the maximum reward among terminals extending prefix p.
Each completed event ends at one of these terminals and reports that table value.
The event sequence can be fully adaptive; no iid model is introduced.

For each node p, D_p is the sum of V(p)-Score over the events actually backed up
through p. Derive D_p=N_p*V(p)-reward_sum_p from the operational backup. When
N_p>0, derive Q_p=V(p)-D_p/N_p. Prove D_p>=0 directly from the finite-table
maximum, and prove terminal-node Q is exact after its first observation.

The result identifies the missing drift quantity without assuming it is small.
In particular, it does not assume D_p=O(log N_p), an MGF bound, or an eventual
optimism property. Deriving a rate for D_p from recursive UCT selection is G04.

At the root D_root is the actual cumulative terminal-quality gap. Derive the
best-observed training simple gap bound D_root/T from the operational maximum.
This is a conditional connection to any subsequently proved root bound, not a
claim that a local same-cache regret bound controls the root automatically.
