OBJECTIVES-01: training recommendation and distinct-policy physical costs.

Connect root terminal-quality deficit to the actual bestCompleted training
recommendation, including exact optimality once the proved finite coverage
threshold is reached. For cumulative net reward compare a second event history
using its own metered cache trajectory and the same evaluation count. Prove
policy net regret <= root quality deficit + lambda*(actual cold budget + actual
recurring overhead), with lambda>=0. The comparator's cache is not replaced by
the algorithm's cache. If the repeated overhead totals agree, cancel them and
retain just the finite cold budget. These are cumulative objectives; validation
selection, population quality and endpoint J still require separate treatment.
