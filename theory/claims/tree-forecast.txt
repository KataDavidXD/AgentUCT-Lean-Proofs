FORECAST-01: full reference completion bills satisfy the operational proof conditions.

For each legal child, a nonnegative normalized reference distribution weights
the predicted cold bill of every terminal below that child. Bills use the
actual restorable qid-prefix cache after the same completed event history and
fixed nonnegative predicted resource weights. With gamma=beta/C_ref >=0,
the penalty lies in [0,gamma*total predicted workflow resource weight].
After the derived recursive coverage threshold, it is zero for every child.
The reference law may depend on history; it need not equal the execution law.
Neither claim certifies prediction accuracy or expected actual cost. Recurring
action-dependent overhead is not included in the vanishing-penalty statement.
