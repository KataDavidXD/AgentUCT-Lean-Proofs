TOPOLOGY-01: finite legal children and optimal descendant values.

Derive legal children and nodes from a finite prefix-antichain of terminal
workflows, rather than padding each node with invalid actions. Legal children
can differ by node. Prove that every actual projected action is legal when
completed terminals belong to this set, and use the finite subtype of legal
actions as the arm type in local UCT analysis.

For bounded terminal rewards, prove bounded optimal descendant values, child
value no greater than parent value, existence of an optimal child at each
internal node, and an instance-dependent positive separation for nonzero edge
gaps. These are properties of the fixed workflow instance, not of observed
sample means or a changing cache-cost comparator. Prove decreasing remaining
depth for every legal child to support induction over the actual finite tree.
