RATE-STEP-01: operational suboptimal-child counts from child induction bounds.

For the actual finite tree run, suppose a proved induction bound on every
child's cumulative deficit holds for every global prefix of that run. Use the
explicit exploration window to make the best child's backed mean accurate
after a fixed local burn-in. After the cold-cost saturation time the index
has zero cost shift. Derive each positive-gap child's count bound from actual
index maximization and injective prior counts. Do not assume a concentration
event or a bound on the parent's own deficit. The child bounds will be supplied
by depth induction, not asserted as a final theorem assumption.
