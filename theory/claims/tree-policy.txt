POLICY-01: project the actual unvisited-first and index-maximizing decisions.

Construct every local decision from a split of the same global event history.
The before-history, selected child, Q and counts must be the actual replayed
quantities; no independent bandit observation stream is introduced. Restrict
the constructed stream to the finite set of legal children. Prove that the
operational allocation rule implies local unvisited-first initialization and
index maximization, with parent count equal to local time plus the derived
zero/root or one/non-root birth offset. Constraints hold within the observed
history only. No constraint is silently imposed on an arbitrary extension.
