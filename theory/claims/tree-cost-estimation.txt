ESTIMATION-01: connect full/legacy forecasts and error bounds to qid-prefix resources.

Define the uniform-terminal law used by production and prove normalization on
each legal child's nonempty terminal set. Express full reference cold cost as
the weighted sum of marginal cold-resource demands; no resource independence
assumption or normalization of those marginal demands is needed.

For prefix-complete cache semantics, child/terminal demand inclusion makes every
terminal cold bill at least the parent cold bill. Prove that legacy clipped
continuation then equals full reference cold cost minus the common parent bill.
Its ranking equivalence is conditional on no omitted action-dependent overhead.
Sparse checkpoint caches use suffix semantics and need not have this inclusion
property for their execution bill; retain the existing clipping counterexample.

For full bill prediction plus overhead, specialize the proved reference-law
error theorem to actual cold qid-prefix sets. Separate resource prediction,
overhead prediction and reference/execution law mismatch. Input error envelopes
are deterministic conditions, not MAE or automatically calibrated intervals.
Prove common action-independent overhead cancels in the whole allocation rule.
