WINDOW-01: an explicit admissible exploration window.

Let A=max(1,c^2) and, for K children and target visit count m, define
 L = ceil(max(K+1, max(exp(((H+2)^2*m+1)/c^2), (8*K*A+2)^2))).
For c>0 and parent-count offset at most one, prove that [L,2L) satisfies
the two arithmetic conditions in ALLOCATION-01. In particular, there is no
unexplained sufficiently-large constant or assumption that counts diverge.

Combine with the actual bounded adaptive index and completed initialization
to prove each child has more than m visits by local time 2L. This bound is
deliberately loose and exponential in m. Recursive use can produce enormous
depth-dependent delays, which must remain visible in any main theorem.
