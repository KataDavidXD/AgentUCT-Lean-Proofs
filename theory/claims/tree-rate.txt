RATE-01: close depth induction for operational cumulative terminal-quality regret.

Use one positive lower bound delta for nonzero static optimal-value edge gaps
in the finite terminal table. It exists by finiteness; it is not a same-cache
dynamic utility gap. Set K to the largest legal branching factor over the tree.
At depth zero use A=B=0. Given A_d,B_d, let tau_d be the largest nodeRateBurnin
over tree nodes, including the derived cache coverage time, and define
 A_(d+1)=K*(A_d+4*c^2/delta^2),
 B_(d+1)=1+K*(B_d+tau_d+1).
Prove for every valid global history prefix and every node of remaining depth
at most d: D_p <= A_d*log(N_p+1)+B_d. Child rates are discharged by induction.
Then specialize zero-after-cache to the actual full cold forecast definition.
Expose assumptions: finite fixed table, full panel, successful persistent
cache, nonnegative normalized reference law, bounded fixed resource forecasts,
c>0, raw reward backup and unvisited-first/index-max allocation. Constants do
not depend on the history length. This is terminal-quality regret, not endpoint J.
