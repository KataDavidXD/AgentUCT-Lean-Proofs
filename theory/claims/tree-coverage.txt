COVERAGE-01: finite recursive coverage of actual tree visits and qid-prefix cache.

Define an explicit, conservative threshold using finite topology and depth:
 C(0)=1,
 C(d+1)=1+max_(p in workflowNodes) 2*explorationWindowStart(c,H,K_p,C(d)).
For a finite prefix-antichain terminal set and a BoundedTreeRun with c>0,
N_p >= C(d) and remaining depth <= d imply every terminal below p has positive
actual backed count. At the root N_root equals completed evaluations T.
Thus T >= C(total depth) covers all terminal demands in the SAME metered run.
Derive physical/materialized and restorable cache statements from the successful
persistent transition model, rather than assuming a cache-saturation time.
These constants can be very large and are not practical budget recommendations.
