RECOMMENDATION-01: actual training shortlist and validation selection.

The production RAG endpoint deduplicates observed terminal paths, ranks by
training reward (then first iteration/path), takes k>=1, and maximizes validation
score inside that shortlist. Model the sorted observed ranking contract; prove
the head is a best observed training workflow and remains in every nonempty
top-k prefix. The trace auditor checks the Python ranking/tie contract.

If training and validation tables differ uniformly by at most epsilon on the
finite workflow class, prove validation simple gap <= D_root/T+2*epsilon. This
is a deterministic transfer bound, not a claim that epsilon has statistical
coverage. Without a table relationship, complete training coverage can still
omit the validation optimum: prove the two-workflow top-1 counterexample.
For endpoint J=quality-lambda*C_opt/C_ref, prove the exact gap decomposition;
equal optimal quality leaves a positive fixed J gap when sunk cost is larger.
No population/iid guarantee is claimed by these finite-table theorems.
