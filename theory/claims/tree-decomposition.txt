DECOMPOSITION-01: actual parent loss splits into birth loss, edge gaps, and child loss.

For the finite legal workflow tree and the SAME global event history, prove
 D_p = birthLoss_p + sum_(a legal at p) [N_(p,a)*(V_p-V_(p,a)) + D_(p,a)].
Here all N and D are the operational quantities already derived from backup.
birthLoss sums V_p-Score only when the expanded endpoint equals p. At an internal
node it is at most one bounded sample, and at the nonterminal root it is zero.

This is an exact terminal-quality decomposition. It is not a sum of the prior
dynamic same-cache local net regrets. The child-action partition must be proved
from prefixes and legal terminal membership, not assumed as an extra recurrence.
