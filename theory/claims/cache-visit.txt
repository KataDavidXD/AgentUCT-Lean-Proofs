CACHEVISIT-01: successful full-panel visits make next-edge selection penalties zero.

In the same persistent execution trace, prove every completed event's full
qid-prefix demand is contained in the final ledger. If a tree prefix has a
positive backed-up count, some completed terminal extends it. Hence its full
panel prefix demand is already materialized, even for a nonempty execution root.

The production immediate estimator is max(0, cold(child)-cold(parent)). Deduce
that this value is zero for any visited child when resource weights are
nonnegative. This is a consequence of the operational backup/cache coupling,
not an assumption that estimated next costs happen to be zero.

Fully expanded selection compares visited children, so next-edge degeneracy
follows in the fixed full-panel persistent setting. It does not imply zero
unexplored descendant cost, zero expansion/rollout penalties, or zero recurring
overhead. A changing qid panel or sparse/deleted cache changes the premises.
