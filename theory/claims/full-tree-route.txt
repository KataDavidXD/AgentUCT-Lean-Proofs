Outstanding structural bridge
Define a finite prefix topology and the recursive/local action histories of a
LegalTreeRun. Prove child visits equal pullCount on the node's local action
history. Parent visits equal local child traversals plus birth offset, with
offset 0 at the root and 1 at a non-root internal node. Its birth evaluation is
the one rollout sample before its first local expansion. Prove the actual
unexpanded-first loop gives the unvisited-first initialization rule; existing
successful children have positive visits. Add maximal treeIndex choice to this
operational transition model. Do not simply assume the target regret rate.

Coverage and cache saturation
For a node with K children let F_s(m)=2*explorationWindowStart(c,H,K,m).
A conservative recursive visit requirement is C_leaf=1 and
 C_s = 1 + F_s(max_child C_child).
Prove this from actual child counts. At the root the resulting T_cache ensures
every terminal workflow has been evaluated, hence all demands are cached.
For cold-only complete forecasts (any nonnegative resource predictions), all
candidate penalties then vanish. Candidate-independent future overhead can be
treated as a common index shift, but action-dependent recurring overhead cannot
be removed this way. Relate global and local time explicitly: local time at least
T_cache is sufficient for a local decision to occur after global saturation.

Candidate deterministic induction for cumulative terminal-quality regret
Take a positive gap lower bound delta for nonzero optimal-value edge gaps in the
fixed finite tree. This is a reward-value gap, not a dynamic same-cache net gap.
Suppose child deficits satisfy D_i(n)<=A_i*log(n+1)+B_i from induction on depth,
including D_i(0)=0. Let A=max A_i, B=max B_i. A deliberately loose target
 m = ceil(max(1, (32*A/delta)^2 + 8*B/delta))
would ensure D_i(n)/n<=delta/4 once n>=m, using log(n+1)<=4*sqrt(n).
This numerical implication itself still needs a Lean proof.

Set local burn-in tau=max(T_cache,F_s(m)). After tau, an optimal child has
Q>=V_s-delta/4 and a selected child always has Q<=its own V_child. Once the
cost penalty is a common shift, selection of a positive-gap child implies
 N_child <= 4*c^2*log(n+1)/delta^2.
Use the checked count-injectivity argument for late selections, plus at most
tau early selections. Prove the exact recursive deficit identity
 D_s(n)=birthLoss_s + sum_i [N_i*Delta_i + D_i(N_i)],
where 0<=birthLoss_s<=1 and child counts sum to n minus the birth offset.
Do not substitute the old sum of dynamic local regrets for this identity.

A conservative candidate recursion is
 A_s = K*(A + 4*c^2/delta^2),
 B_s = 1 + K*(B + tau + 1),
with A_leaf=B_leaf=0. Verify all indexing, initialization and log shifts before
accepting these constants. They can have enormous nested-exponential dependence
through F_s and the recursive visit targets; disclose them rather than writing
an unexplained instance-dependent constant. The index permits the actual c=1.4;
it requires c>0, not c=sqrt(2), in this deterministic finite-table main result.

Later G05 connection
If the root bound is established, its D_root is actual cumulative terminal-
quality regret. best-training simple gap <= D_root/T is already checked.
For cumulative net reward, compare another policy using its own cache ledger;
nonnegative comparator cost plus the finite cold budget gives an additive
lambda*B_cache term, with repeated overhead kept explicitly. This is distinct
from endpoint J and validation-shortlist selection. Endpoint J can retain a
constant cost gap even if training quality converges; it is not automatically
optimized by a logarithmic cumulative-quality bound.
